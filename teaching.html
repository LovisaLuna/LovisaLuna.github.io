Teaching philosophy

Learning through discussion and exploration

I see learning as an active and collaborative process. Discussion, experimentation, and opportunities to work through problems together can help students develop both understanding and confidence. I therefore aim to create teaching environments where students are encouraged to ask questions, test ideas, and engage actively with the material.

I also believe that effective teaching starts from where students are. Students come with different backgrounds, experiences, and ways of approaching a problem, and I try to meet them at their current level while helping them develop further. As I continue developing as a teacher, I am particularly interested in finding ways to make technical subjects accessible without sacrificing depth.
