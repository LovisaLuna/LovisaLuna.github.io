SciFest

Experimenting with AI

I have participated in SciFest in 2024 and 2025, developing interactive activities around machine learning and generative AI, focusing especially on trustworthiness, creativity, and risks .

2025

SciFest Uppsala

In 2025, our activity focused on experimenting with generative AI through workshops, creative challenges, and discussions. During the school days, student groups competed in creative image-generation challenges and explored what AI could and could not do, as well as discussed what it should and should not do. The public day was more open-ended, with visitors experimenting freely and taking part in spontaneous challenges.

2024

SciFest Uppsala

In 2024, I participated with an activity encouraging visitors to think critically about how they interact with chatbots, and to what extent answers can be trusted.

An activity

Can you trust a chatbot?

We gave visitors questions and challenges designed to explore what chatbots know, what they get wrong, and how much control users really have over them.

  • Can it be trusted? Ask it about Mahasti Saghafi, Rbukha, or stenmurkla.
  • Can it keep a secret? Try to make it reveal a secret word.
  • Can you control it? Teach it the invented language Karimto.

The full activity included three exercises about chatbot reliability, privacy, and how much users can control an AI.
